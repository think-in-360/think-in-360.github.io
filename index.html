Abstract

PanoVideoBench

PanoVideoBench contains 1,000 manually annotated four-choice questions from 223 panoramic videos, spanning 23.06 h. The questions cover five categories and 15 question types, with diagnostic annotations for target search and panoramic capabilities.

PanoVideoCoT

PanoVideoCoT contains 13,465 QA–CoT pairs from 2,063 real panoramic videos, spanning 168.8 hours. The corpus includes verified sphere-time evidence and approximately 100K evidence nodes.

Main results

PanoVideoReasoner reaches 55.7% on PanoVideoBench, improving its Qwen3.5-9B backbone by 12.5 points and the previous SOTA by 2.6 points.
